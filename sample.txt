The Old Barn

There was an old barn on my grandpa's farm. Everyone said it was haunted. One night me and my cousin Jake decided to go in. It was very dark and very scary. We heard a noise. It was a really creepy noise. Jake said "what was that" and I said "I don't know". Then we saw a ghost. The ghost was white and it floated. We were very scared so we ran away. The next day grandpa told us a farmer died in that barn in 1952. We never went back.
